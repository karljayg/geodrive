# Soundtrack

Made by the owner (karljayg) with Suno, from the Drive folder shared on 2026-11-02. Each file keeps its Drive name's number and title, in web-safe form. The number is the mood slot: 1 daytime, 2 late afternoon, 3 not made yet (assumed dusk), 4 night, 5 foreboding, 6 suspense, 7 danger, 8 chase, 9 not made yet.

Each song's `volume` in `public/js/musicConfig.js` evens it out to about -17 LUFS, measured with ffmpeg's loudnorm filter. They aren't re-encoded.
